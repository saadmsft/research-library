# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
The owner (a self-directed researcher) uses the site first as a private study library: reading, re-reading and adding research. After that it is published publicly so other readers can read the research. Public readers arrive curious about a specific topic (the first is Sufi breathwork in the Qadiri silsila) and want to read a thorough paper, check its sources, and move to related papers.

## Product Purpose
A personal research library in which every investigation is a full paper filed inside a collection (a folder). One site holds many researches across different fields, so a reader can browse by collection and read each paper in depth, with visual explanation alongside the text. Success: the owner can add a new paper by dropping a Markdown file into a collection folder, and a reader can find, read and trust a paper.

## Positioning
This is a library of structured papers, not a blog feed. Each paper separates what is verified in primary sources from what is only claimed or unverified, and shows its sources. Collections are real folders with real structure.

## Operating Context
- Static site with no framework and no backend.
- Markdown files organised in collection folders are turned into pages by a small build script.
- Intended to be hosted free (for example GitHub Pages).
- Papers carry citations and quoted passages, and include Arabic, Persian and Urdu terms and script alongside English.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Collections (folders) contain papers; papers may be grouped into series.
- Each paper: title, summary, date, status, sources, and long-form body with visual aids (diagrams, charts, timelines, evidence tables).
- Content will be mixed over time: study notes, spirituality, health and other research.
- Undecided: site name, domain, search implementation, comments or sharing features.

## Brand Commitments
None confirmed. The name and identity are open.

## Evidence on Hand
Two researched reports from this project's origin conversation become the first papers: a general report on Pas Anfas (pas-i anfas, "guarding the breath") across Sufi orders, and a Qadiri-focused report on Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani's teaching and the Qadiri lineage, checked against primary translations. No testimonials, readership numbers or institutional affiliations exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. A paper is a first-class object: complete, sourced, readable at length.
2. Verified and unverified claims are always visibly distinguished.
3. Folders are real structure; adding research means adding a file.
4. Visuals explain the research; they never decorate it or replace the text.
5. Respect the subject matter: no exoticism, no borrowed sacred ornament as decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Long-form reading must be comfortable on phones and desktops. Arabic, Persian and Urdu script must render correctly (right-to-left, suitable fonts) inline within English text.
